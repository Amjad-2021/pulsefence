PulseFence

A low-cost intelligent system for protecting smart-city networks against spoofed wireless signals

Future vision

  • 1Validate replay detection on real hardware

    Record replays of our own sensors with low-cost software radios (RTL-SDR, HackRF) and test PulseNet on them. The public WiSig data has no replays, so today replay detection is shown in simulation and impostor detection on real data.

  • 2Run on a low-cost edge node

    Deploy the ~20 KB model on a Raspberry Pi with 4 receivers, add the location check, and raise single-node detection (74% today) by sharing evidence between neighbouring nodes.

  • 3Scale to a whole city

    Test with 150 devices (WiSig ManyTx), counter close lab-grade attackers with angle-of-arrival antennas, and share learning between districts with federated learning.
